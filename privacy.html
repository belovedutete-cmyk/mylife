MY LIFE Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

The short version

  • MY LIFE is a personal organiser for your day, health, money and faith. Your data is used only to run the app for you.
  • We never sell your data, never show adverts and never use your data to train AI models.
  • Your data is stored in London (UK) and protected so that only your account can read it.
  • Bank and investment data comes in only if you connect an account, and you can disconnect at any time.
  • You can delete your account and all of your data inside the app: Settings → Privacy & Security → Delete account.

1. Who we are

MY LIFE is made by Beloved Tariro Utete, a sole trader in the United Kingdom (“we”, “us”). We are the data controller for the personal data described in this policy.

Contact: Lasprod.official@gmail.com

2. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: email address, password (stored only as a secure hash by our database provider) and the display name you choose.
  • Profile: optional workplace name and area, home label, time zone and shift times.
  • Your content: notes, tasks, categories, priorities, budget lines, savings figures, Bible reading progress and anything else you type into the app.
  • Spending you add: transactions you enter yourself, statements you import, and purchases recorded by the optional Apple Pay Shortcut.
  • Assistant chats: if you use the AI assistant (MY LIFE Plus), the messages you send and the replies.

Information from services you choose to connect

  • Bank and investment accounts: if you connect Monzo, NatWest, Starling or Trading 212, we receive account names, balances and transactions (date, amount, description, merchant and category) for the accounts you approve. We never receive your banking password or card numbers.
  • Apple Wallet (FinanceKit): if this becomes available in MY LIFE and you allow it, the balances and transactions you choose to share from Wallet.

Information that stays on your iPhone

  • Calendar: with your permission, MY LIFE reads calendar events on your iPhone to show your day and work out your shift. Events are not uploaded to our servers. New events are only added through Apple’s own “Add event” screen, after you confirm.
  • Reminders: with your permission, MY LIFE keeps your tasks and Apple Reminders in sync. Reminders you choose to sync are saved as tasks in your account.

Activity record

To help you see what has changed, we keep an activity log of actions on your account (for example “Note created”, “Asked the assistant”). It records what changed and when, never the content of your notes, tasks or messages. You can view it in Settings → Privacy & Security.

We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking tools, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

3. How we use your information

  • To provide the app: store and sync your content, show your day, budget, spending and savings.
  • To import data from accounts you connect, and keep it up to date.
  • To answer your questions with the AI assistant, if you use it.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent abuse (for example assistant usage limits) and fix problems.
  • To manage MY LIFE Plus subscriptions, if you buy one.

Legal bases (UK GDPR): providing the service you signed up for (contract); your consent for connecting bank accounts, Apple Wallet, Calendar and Reminders, which you can withdraw at any time; and our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure.

Some content you add may reveal sensitive information, such as health notes or religious beliefs (for example Bible reading). This is stored only to provide the features you use, with your explicit consent, and is never used for anything else.

5. How long we keep it

  • Your data is kept while your account exists.
  • When you delete your account, your account and all of its data, including bank connections, assistant chats and the activity log, are deleted from our database straight away. Copies in our provider’s encrypted backups are overwritten within its backup cycle (normally within 7 days).
  • Bank access expires automatically when your bank consent ends (UK banks ask you to renew every 90 days), or sooner if you disconnect.

6. How we protect it

  • All connections are encrypted (HTTPS/TLS), and data is encrypted at rest by our database provider.
  • Row-level security means each account can only ever read its own data.
  • Your sign-in session is kept in the iPhone Keychain.
  • Secret keys (for the AI assistant and bank connections) are kept only on the server, never in the app.
  • The AI assistant can read the context described above to answer you, but it cannot create, change or delete anything.

7. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your data, object to or restrict how we use it, and withdraw consent at any time. You can:

  • edit or delete your content in the app;
  • disconnect bank accounts in Settings → Connections, and remove MY LIFE from your banking app’s connected apps;
  • turn off Calendar or Reminders access in the iPhone Settings app;
  • delete your account in Settings → Privacy & Security → Delete account;
  • email us at Lasprod.official@gmail.com for anything else. We will reply within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

8. Children

MY LIFE is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. If the change is significant, we will also tell you in the app.
